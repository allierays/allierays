I believe real-world experience beats a certification every time, but it's nice to validate your skills in something that is changing and growing this fast. We might not need to learn code syntax anymore. Making real solution architecture decisions at scale will always be the human factor, and it's the difference between something vibe coded and something that can withstand enterprise production.

I helped my friend Eddie Belaval and [id8Labs](https://id8labs.app/) put together a cohort to prep for the Claude Certified Architect - Professional exam. It grew to 74 people. We had a WhatsApp group and met up to talk through each domain of the course.

There is no official mock exam, so I used Claude to build practice questions and a mock exam for myself and the cohort. After I sat the exam, I reviewed the questions and tweaked the format.

This practice exam isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. It's what I used to prepare, and I'm sharing it in case it helps you too.
