I didn't study for the Claude Certified Architect - Professional exam alone.

I helped my friend Eddie Belaval and id8Labs put together a cohort to study for the exam and take it together. It grew to 74 people. We had a WhatsApp group and met up to talk through each domain of the course.

There is no official mock exam, so I used Claude to build practice questions and a mock exam for myself and the cohort. After I sat the exam, I reviewed the questions and tweaked the format.

I have no official ties to Anthropic. This is what I used to prepare, and I'm sharing it in case it helps you too.
