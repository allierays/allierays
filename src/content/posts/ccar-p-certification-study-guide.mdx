Experience beats a certification every time. Still, when the field moves this fast, it's worth checking your skills against a standard. If AI can write the syntax now, what's still worth learning? The architecture decisions that separate a vibe-coded demo from a system that survives enterprise production. That's what the Claude Certified Architect - Professional certification tests.

I helped my friend Eddie Belaval and [id8Labs](https://id8labs.app/) put together a cohort to prep for the certification. It grew to 74 people. We had a WhatsApp group and met up to talk through each domain of the course.

There is no official mock exam, so I used Claude to build practice questions and a mock exam for the cohort. After I passed the exam, I reviewed the questions and tweaked the format.

I'm sharing it in case it helps you too. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
